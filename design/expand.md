# ASGARD: REALMS OF THE GODS
## Minecraft Mod — Expansion Design Document

**Theme:** Norse Mythology, Divine Powers, Ancient Magic, Exploration, Legendary Artifacts  
**Core Elements:** Yggdrasil, Asgard, Thor's Shrine, Potions of the Gods  
**Design Philosophy:** Make Norse mythology feel like a natural but extraordinary extension of Minecraft.

---

# 1. Core Vision

Expand the existing Asgard mod into a complete mythological experience featuring:

- Yggdrasil, the World Tree.
- Divine shrines dedicated to the Norse gods.
- Legendary weapons and armor.
- Potions and blessings from the gods.
- Mythological creatures and companions.
- Ancient Norse ruins and temples.
- The Nine Realms.
- Divine bosses and challenging encounters.
- Unique crafting materials and progression.

Players should gradually discover the mythology, earn the favor of the gods, and eventually become powerful enough to challenge the forces threatening the Nine Realms.

# 2. YGGDRASIL — The World Tree

Yggdrasil should be one of the most recognizable features of the mod.

### The Great Tree
- Enormous custom tree structure.
- Massive trunk with ancient glowing runes.
- Roots extending underground into caves.
- Branches reaching high into the sky.
- Glowing leaves with occasional falling golden particles.
- Mystical birds living among the branches.
- Ancient shrines scattered around its roots.

### Special Yggdrasil Blocks
- Yggdrasil Wood.
- Yggdrasil Leaves.
- Ancient World Tree Bark.
- Golden World Tree Leaves.
- Yggdrasil Sap.
- Sacred Roots.
- World Tree Seeds.
- Yggdrasil Heartwood.

### Yggdrasil Sap
A rare crafting ingredient collected from special parts of the tree.

Used for:
- Divine potions.
- Regeneration elixirs.
- Sacred crafting recipes.
- Upgrading god weapons.
- Creating magical artifacts.

### The Three Wells

Three magical wells surround the roots of Yggdrasil.

**Well of Urd**
- Associated with fate and destiny.
- Grants experience and temporary luck.
- Used to craft enchantments.

**Well of Mimir**
- Associated with wisdom.
- Grants night vision and knowledge-related abilities.
- Used for advanced magical crafting.

**Hvergelmir**
- An ancient primordial spring.
- Produces rare magical water.
- Used in powerful potions and divine rituals.

| Potion | Effect |
|---|---|
| Thor's Fury | Strength and lightning resistance |
| Odin's Wisdom | Increased experience gain |
| Freyja's Grace | Regeneration and resistance |
| Loki's Deception | Invisibility and speed |
| Freyr's Blessing | Increased crop growth |
| Tyr's Courage | Strength and knockback resistance |
| Heimdall's Sight | Night vision and glowing entities |
| Idunn's Elixir | Powerful regeneration |

- Yggdrasil Sap.

**Trolls**

**Draugr Lords**

**Valkyries**

# 7. THE NINE REALMS

Eventually expand the mod into different Norse-inspired dimensions.

Each realm should have its own terrain, structures, creatures, resources, and progression.

| Realm | Environment | Features |
|---|---|---|
| Asgard | Golden heavenly kingdom | Divine temples and gods |
| Midgard | Overworld | Shrines and ancient ruins |
| Vanaheim | Magical forests | Nature magic and rare plants |
| Alfheim | Glowing enchanted forests | Light elves and crystals |
| Svartalfheim | Underground kingdoms | Dwarven forging and machinery |
| Jotunheim | Frozen mountains | Frost giants and ice structures |
| Muspelheim | Volcanic wasteland | Fire giants and flame materials |
| Niflheim | Frozen mist realm | Ancient ice creatures |
| Helheim | Dark underworld | Draugr and ancient undead |

These could be introduced gradually rather than all at once.

Players collect ancient runes and use them to modify weapons, armor, and equipment.

### Rune Types

| Rune | Ability |
|---|---|
| Fehu | Increased resource drops |
| Uruz | Increased strength |
| Thurisaz | Increased attack damage |
| Ansuz | Increased experience |
| Raidho | Increased movement speed |
| Kenaz | Fire resistance |
| Algiz | Defensive protection |
| Sowilo | Increased light and energy |
| Berkano | Regeneration |
| Tiwaz | Combat bonuses |

### Rune Inscription Table

A new workstation where players:
1. Place an item.
2. Insert a rune.
3. Supply magical essence.
4. Apply the rune.

Rare runes could be found in temples and boss encounters.

# 12. DIVINE EVENTS

Add occasional world events that make the mythology feel alive.

**The Wild Hunt**
- A group of spectral riders appears at night.
- They travel through forests.
- Rare rewards for defeating them.

**Thor's Storm**
- A special thunderstorm occurs.
- Lightning strikes nearby.
- Rare storm materials become available.

**The Bifrost**
- A rainbow bridge briefly appears in the sky.
- Leads to a temporary structure or encounter.

**Odin's Ravens**
- Two ravens appear near the player.
- They can lead players toward nearby ancient structures.

**Fimbulwinter**
- An unusual cold weather event.
- Snow spreads across certain areas.
- Special frost creatures appear.

# 16. PROGRESSION SYSTEM

The player gradually becomes a champion of the gods.

**Stage 1 — Mortal**
- Discover ancient shrines.
- Find basic runes.
- Craft initial divine potions.

**Stage 2 — Favored**
- Earn blessings from individual gods.
- Discover Yggdrasil.
- Collect divine materials.
- Craft initial legendary equipment.

**Stage 3 — Champion**
- Explore ancient Nordic structures.
- Defeat Draugr Lords and giants.
- Access dwarven forging.
- Craft divine armor.

**Stage 4 — Godslayer**
- Explore the Nine Realms.
- Defeat Fenrir and Nidhogg.
- Acquire legendary weapons.
- Challenge the major gods and primordial beings.

# 18. DESIGN PRINCIPLES

1. Every god should have a recognizable visual identity.
2. Every structure should tell a story.
3. Every rare item should have a meaningful use.
4. Every creature should have unique behavior.
5. Divine equipment should feel genuinely special.
6. Exploration should be rewarded.
7. New mechanics should complement vanilla Minecraft rather than replace it.
8. The mod should be modular so new realms, gods, and creatures can be added over time.

**Ultimate Vision:** Create a complete Norse mythology experience in Minecraft, where Yggdrasil connects the worlds, the gods influence the Overworld, ancient creatures roam the wilderness, and players can eventually become legends themselves.
