Structures

Norse structures in the world

Three structures generate in new worlds. Each hides a relic in its chest. Walk, look, and bring a sword.

Yggdrasil

A giant hollow tree, 41 blocks across and 50 high, with roots, branches, a canopy cut by shafts of sunlight and lanterns hung beneath it. A doorway at the foot leads to a chest inside.

Yggdrasil: facts
Size41 by 41 blocks, 50 high (15,979 blocks)
SpacingOne in every 40 by 40 chunks, at least 12 chunks from the next
Biomestaiga, forest
ChestThe hollow holds the Gjallarhorn, fruit, leaves and a golden apple. 1 in 4 chests (25%) hold the relic.

Thor’s shrine

A stone dais with four lightning-rod pillars, a spire, a gold block altar and a chest. Lightning strikes the rods in a storm.

Thor’s shrine: facts
Size13 by 13 blocks, 12 high (330 blocks)
SpacingOne in every 28 by 28 chunks, at least 10 chunks from the next
Biomesmountain, hill, plains, forest
ChestThe altar chest holds Mjölnir, metal and rune stone. 2 in 5 chests (40%) hold the relic.

Rune circle

Eight rune-stone pillars around a mossy floor, a chest and a soul lantern at the centre.

Rune circle: facts
Size13 by 13 blocks, 6 high (155 blocks)
SpacingOne in every 20 by 20 chunks, at least 8 chunks from the next
Biomestaiga, forest, hill, plains
ChestThe chest holds Gungnir, ingredients for the gods’ potions and rune stone. 1 in 6 chests (17%) hold the relic.

Status: all three generate with the game’s /place structure command on a Minecraft 1.21.4 server, and the captures above are from that client in a flat test world (the stone, trees and sky are vanilla). That the structures appear on their own in a fresh survival world is not yet confirmed: the game’s /locate found nothing near spawn on the test seed, and the same search for a vanilla village also found nothing, so that test proved neither way.
