Structures

Norse structures in the world

Three structures generate in new worlds. Each hides a relic in its chest. Walk, look, and bring a sword. Yggdrasil is the largest.

Yggdrasil

The world tree: a buttressed trunk with a gold rune spiral, root ridges that run out along the ground, sprawling branches under raised leaf clusters, a walkway with lanterns along one branch, vines, hanging lanterns and glowing fruit, and two waterfalls falling into stone-ringed pools. An amethyst hollow at its foot has a doorway and a chest, and a ladder climbs the trunk from the hollow to Valhalla, a hall on the crown with a chest of its own. Six variants are in the world pool, so no two trees need match.

Yggdrasil: facts
Size85 by 85 blocks, 100 high (about 61,600 blocks, six variants)
SpacingOne in every 40 by 40 chunks, at least 12 chunks from the next
Biomestaiga, forest
ChestThe hollow holds the Gjallarhorn, fruit, leaves and a golden apple. Valhalla, up the ladder, holds a relic more often. 1 in 4 chests (25%) hold the relic.

Thor’s shrine

A three-tier stone dais with four lightning-rod pillars, glowing rune inlays, braziers at the four sides and a tall lightning-rod spire over a gold altar and chest. Three variants: one pillar is broken in each, and the stone is cracked and mossy to different degrees.

Thor’s shrine: facts
Size21 by 21 blocks, 17 high (about 1,090 blocks)
SpacingOne in every 28 by 28 chunks, at least 10 chunks from the next
Biomesmountain, hill, plains, forest
ChestThe altar chest holds Mjölnir, metal and rune stone. 2 in 5 chests (40%) hold the relic.

Rune circle

Eight to eleven rune-stone pillars of uneven height around a floor of mossy stone, a glowing rune ring, soul lanterns on some capitals and a chest at the centre. Three variants; some pillars have fallen.

Rune circle: facts
Size17 by 17 blocks, 9 high (about 275 blocks)
SpacingOne in every 20 by 20 chunks, at least 8 chunks from the next
Biomestaiga, forest, hill, plains
ChestThe chest holds Gungnir, ingredients for the gods’ potions and rune stone. 1 in 6 chests (17%) hold the relic.

Advancements

The mod adds a small tree of advancements: A Leaf for the Blessing, Restless Dead, A Garden of Gold and Crystal, The Harvest Lord, Heimdall’s Watch, The Spear That Never Misses, The Watchman’s Tower, Under the Ground, Smiling, Worthy, The Allfather’s Monument, Hugin or Munin, Standing Stones, Thor’s Threshold, Under the Bridge, Where the Oath Was Kept, Beneath the World Tree.

Status: the structures generate with the game’s /place structure command on a Minecraft 1.21.4 server (Yggdrasil, the shrine and the circle were each placed that way; the variants and Valhalla’s ladder and chest were placed as templates for the captures, and nobody has climbed the ladder yet). The advancements load without errors but none has been earned in play, and the captures above are from that client in a flat test world (the stone, trees and sky are vanilla). That the structures appear on their own in a fresh survival world is not yet confirmed: the game’s /locate found nothing near spawn on the test seed, and the same search for a vanilla village also found nothing, so that test proved neither way.
