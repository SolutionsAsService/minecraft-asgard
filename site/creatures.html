Creatures

Draugr, trolls and ravens

The first three creatures of the design’s Phase 2. They reuse Minecraft’s zombie and parrot models with skins painted for the mod.

Draugr

An undead Norse warrior in a rusted iron helm, with a glowing blue stare. It is tougher and slower than a zombie, never burns in sunlight, and carries an iron axe or sword. It uses the zombie model with its own skin.

Draugr: facts
Stats34 health (17 hearts), 5 attack damage, 6 armour, knockback resistance 0.3
Spawnstaiga, forest, mountain biomes; groups of 1 to 3, spawn weight 14. Also haunts the rune circle and Tyr’s shrine.
DropsBone, Rotten Flesh, Iron Nugget, Rune Stone, Yggdrasil Sap, Iron Ingot
Spawn eggIn the Spawn Eggs tab, and /summon asgard:draugr

Troll

A hulking mountain troll, one and a half times a player’s size, with stone patches, moss and tusks. It hits hard and shrugs off knockback, and in open daylight it turns to stone: it stops moving and attacking until night, or until it is under cover. It uses the zombie model, scaled up.

Troll: facts
Stats70 health (35 hearts), 10 attack damage, 4 armour, knockback resistance 0.7, 1.5 times scale
Spawnsmountain, taiga biomes; one at a time, spawn weight 4. Also guards Tyr’s shrine, rarely.
DropsCobblestone, Rotten Flesh, Bone, Iron Ingot, Rune Stone, Sacred Root, Emerald
Spawn eggIn the Spawn Eggs tab, and /summon asgard:troll

Raven

Odin’s black bird. It flies, can be tamed with seeds, and mimics the sounds around it like a parrot. It uses the parrot model with a black skin.

Raven: facts
StatsThe parrot’s: 6 health, flies, tameable with seeds.
Spawnstaiga, forest, mountain biomes; groups of 2 to 4, spawn weight 6. Lives around Yggdrasil and Odin’s shrine.
DropsFeather, Ender Pearl
Spawn eggIn the Spawn Eggs tab, and /summon asgard:raven

Not built yet

The design also lists Ratatoskr, other mythical beasts, companions and bosses. Creatures with their own models and animations need custom model code and are planned for later.

Status: all three load and render in a Minecraft 1.21.4 client when summoned, and the first batch dropped bones, iron nuggets, rune stones, feathers and cobblestone when it was killed, so the loot tables work. Their natural spawn rules and the troll’s daylight petrification are written but not yet watched over a full day and night, and no advancement has been earned in play.
