Creatures

Draugr, trolls, giants, wolves and a raven

Seven creatures from the design’s Phase 2, every one with its own hand-built model (20 to 45 parts) and a texture painted from the same description. The six that walk are defined as data in mobs/*.json and generated by the shadw-mod mob tool; the raven is a hand-written parrot that takes only its model from the tool.

Draugr

An undead Norse warrior in a rusted iron cap, a braided beard and a torn blue cape, with glowing ice-blue eyes, a round shield and a notched sword. Tougher and slower than a zombie, and it never burns in sunlight.

Draugr: facts
Stats34 health (17 hearts), 5 attack damage, 6 armour, knockback resistance 0.3
Spawnstaiga, old growth pine taiga, old growth spruce taiga, snowy taiga, forest, birch forest, windswept hills, windswept forest, jagged peaks, stony peaks, frozen peaks, snowy slopes, grove; groups of 1 to 3, spawn weight 14. Also haunts the rune circle and Tyr’s shrine.
DropsBone, Rotten Flesh, Iron Nugget, Rune Stone, Yggdrasil Sap, Iron Ingot
Spawn eggIn the Spawn Eggs tab, and /summon asgard:draugr

Draugr Lord

The mound-king: taller than a draugr, in a horned helm with iron pauldrons, a red cape, a shield and a greataxe. Its blows weaken you, and when it is nearly dead it goes into a rage.

Draugr Lord: facts
Stats90 health (45 hearts), 8 attack damage, 10 armour, knockback resistance 0.5, 1.2 times scale. Behaviour: heavy knockback, enrages when badly hurt, its hits cause weakness
Spawnsold growth pine taiga, old growth spruce taiga, snowy taiga, windswept hills, jagged peaks, stony peaks; one at a time, spawn weight 2.
DropsBone, Iron Ingot, Rune Stone, Diamond, Yggdrasil Sap
Spawn eggIn the Spawn Eggs tab, and /summon asgard:draugr_lord

Troll

A hulking mountain troll, a head and a half taller than a player when it stands, but it stoops: long arms, a heavy jaw and tusks, moss on its head and stone growing from its back, a club at its side. It hits hard and shrugs off knockback, and in open daylight it turns to stone: it stops moving and attacking until night, or until it is under cover.

Troll: facts
Stats70 health (35 hearts), 10 attack damage, 4 armour, knockback resistance 0.7, 1.4 times scale. Behaviour: turns to stone in open daylight, heavy knockback
Spawnswindswept hills, windswept forest, jagged peaks, stony peaks, frozen peaks, snowy slopes, grove, taiga, old growth pine taiga, old growth spruce taiga, snowy taiga; one at a time, spawn weight 4. Also guards Tyr’s shrine, rarely.
DropsCobblestone, Rotten Flesh, Bone, Iron Ingot, Rune Stone, Sacred Root, Emerald
Spawn eggIn the Spawn Eggs tab, and /summon asgard:troll

Jötunn

A frost giant of Jötunheimr: ice-blue skin, a white beard to the belt, a crown of ice spikes, a fur cloak and a hammer of ice. Its blows slow you, and it stamps the ground around it.

Jötunn: facts
Stats140 health (70 hearts), 12 attack damage, 6 armour, knockback resistance 0.8, 1.6 times scale. Behaviour: ground stamp, heavy knockback, its hits cause slowness
Spawnssnowy slopes, frozen peaks, jagged peaks, grove, snowy taiga; one at a time, spawn weight 1.
DropsIce, Packed Ice, Bone, Rune Stone, Diamond
Spawn eggIn the Spawn Eggs tab, and /summon asgard:jotunn

Warg

A big black wolf of the northern forests, with a ridge down its spine, amber eyes and bared fangs. It is fast, hunts in packs at night, and pounces: it bursts into a short sprint from a few blocks away.

Warg: facts
Stats30 health (15 hearts), 6 attack damage, 2 armour, knockback resistance 0.2, 1 times scale. Behaviour: pounce / charge
Spawnstaiga, old growth pine taiga, old growth spruce taiga, snowy taiga, snowy plains, forest, windswept hills, grove, snowy slopes; groups of 2 to 4, spawn weight 8.
DropsBone, Leather, Rotten Flesh, Rune Stone
Spawn eggIn the Spawn Eggs tab, and /summon asgard:warg

Fenrir, the Bound Wolf

Fenrir, the wolf who bit off Tyr’s hand and broke every chain the gods made for him. A boss: huge, silver-grey, with a mane like frost, scarred, red-eyed, still wearing the broken manacles and trailing links of chain. He charges, stamps, and when he is half dead he goes into a rage. He has a boss bar.

Fenrir, the Bound Wolf: facts
Stats320 health (160 hearts), 14 attack damage, 8 armour, knockback resistance 1, 2.4 times scale. Behaviour: boss bar, enrages when badly hurt, pounce / charge, ground stamp, heavy knockback
Spawnsfrozen peaks, jagged peaks, snowy slopes; one at a time, spawn weight 1. Rare: one at a time, only on the highest snowy peaks, and at night like any monster.
DropsBone, Diamond, Netherite Scrap, Rune Stone, Sacred Root, Yggdrasil Sap
Spawn eggIn the Spawn Eggs tab, and /summon asgard:fenrir

Raven

Odin’s black bird, with a blue sheen on its feathers, a heavy beak, a ruffled throat and a fan of tail feathers. It flies, can be tamed with seeds, and mimics the sounds around it like a parrot. It is a parrot underneath, with its own model; the wings beat in the air and fold when it lands.

Raven: facts
StatsThe parrot’s: 6 health, flies, tameable with seeds.
SpawnsTaiga, forest and mountain biomes, groups of 2 to 4. Lives around Yggdrasil and Odin’s shrine.
DropsFeather, Ender Pearl
Spawn eggIn the Spawn Eggs tab, and /summon asgard:raven

Not built yet

The design also lists Ratatoskr, Nidhogg, Sleipnir, Valkyries, Surtr and Hel, forest spirits and companions. Fenrir is a boss you meet on a peak; the design’s underground prison where he breaks his chains is not built.

Status: all seven load and render in a Minecraft 1.21.4 client, and were photographed from six angles each. The six that walk were measured in the game: the draugr reached a player 12 blocks away in 6 s and hit for 3.5; the draugr lord in 6 s for 5 with weakness; the warg in 3 s for 4 and pounced (speed effect seen); the frost giant in 7 s for 7 with slowness; Fenrir in 3 s for 8; the troll stood still at noon and, at midnight, reached the player in 6 s and hit for 6. Each of those is a single run. The drops were seen when each was killed. Animation was checked in still photographs only (the raven’s wing beat in flight and its fold on the ground are written but only the folded pose was photographed), and nothing was hand-played or watched over a full day and night. Fenrir’s boss bar showed in the photographs.
