Shrines

Seven shrines, seven altars

Each god has a shrine in the world and an altar block to make an offering at. Hold the offering, use the altar, and the blessing is yours.

God of Thunder

Thor

The first shrine: a tiered stone dais, lightning-rod pillars, braziers and a spire over a gold altar.

Thor’s shrine: facts
Size21 by 21 blocks, 17 high; 3 variants
SpacingOne in every 28 by 28 chunks
Biomesmountain, hill, plains, forest
AltarHold 1 Lightning Rod and use the altar: Strength I for 5:00, Resistance I for 2:00.
ChestMjolnir, Iron Ingot, Copper Ingot, Gold Ingot, Lightning Rod, Rune Stone, Thunder Iron, Redstone

Allfather

Odin

A stone monolith with glowing runes, a one-eyed statue under a wide hat, two raven statues, and lanterns hanging from carved posts.

Odin’s shrine: facts
Size19 by 19 blocks, 18 high; 2 variants
SpacingOne in every 30 by 30 chunks
Biomesmountain, taiga, windswept hills
AltarHold 1 Ender Eye and use the altar: Night Vision I for 5:00, Luck I for 5:00.
ChestMimir Glass, Ender Eye, Experience Bottle, Gold Ingot, Rune Stone, Yggdrasil Sap, Sacred Root, Potion of All-Father’s Eye, Potion of Mimir’s Knowledge

Goddess of Love and Magic

Freyja

A garden of golden and pink flowers, crystals that glow, a cherry pergola and quartz cat statues.

Freyja’s shrine: facts
Size21 by 21 blocks, 13 high; 2 variants
SpacingOne in every 28 by 28 chunks
Biomesflower forest, meadow, cherry grove, plains, sunflower plains
AltarHold 1 Peony and use the altar: Regeneration II for 0:20, Resistance I for 1:30.
ChestValhalla Honey, Pink Petals, Peony, Amethyst Shard, Gold Ingot, Yggdrasil Sap, Sacred Root, Potion of Hall Mead, Potion of Idunn’s Elixir

God of Mischief

Not built yet

The expansion design also names rewards this phase does not have: Seidr magic and the feathered cloak (Freyja), the Trickster’s Ring (Loki), sacred farming tools and extra crop growth (Freyr), Tyr’s Shield, Bifrost fragments, and Gungnir fragments. They are planned for the later phases.

Status: the shrines were placed in a Minecraft 1.21.4 test world with the game’s template command and photographed from a client; the offering altars load, but no offering has been made in play and no advancement earned. Natural spawning in a fresh survival world is not yet confirmed.
