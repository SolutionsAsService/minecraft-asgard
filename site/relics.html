Relics

Three relics and the world tree

Mjölnir, Gungnir and Gjallarhorn are found in the Norse structures, not crafted. Yggdrasil gives a leaf for one more potion and a fruit to eat.

Mjölnir

Thor’s hammer

  • Heavy weapon: +8 attack damage (9 with the base 1), attack speed modifier -3 (a slow swing), 1561 durability.
  • Right-click: lightning strikes where you look, up to 40 blocks away. Costs 3 durability and has a 3-second cooldown.
  • Found in Thor’s shrine chests: 2 in 5 chests (40%) hold it.

Gungnir

Odin’s spear

  • Spear: +7 attack damage (8 with the base 1), attack speed modifier -2.4, 1200 durability.
  • What it hits glows for 10 seconds, through walls.
  • Found in rune circle chests: 1 in 6 chests (17%) hold it.

Gjallarhorn

Heimdall’s horn

  • Right-click: a horn blast. Everything living within 48 blocks glows for 10 seconds, and you run (Speed II) for 10 seconds.
  • 30-second cooldown, 64 uses.
  • Found in Yggdrasil’s hollow: 1 in 4 chests (25%) hold it.

Yggdrasil Fruit

Fruit of the world tree

  • Food: restores 4 hunger. A little saturation (0.3), nothing else.
  • Dropped by Yggdrasil leaves (5% of the time) and found in its hollow.

Yggdrasil Leaf

Ingredient

  • Brew it onto an Awkward Potion for the Potion of Blessing: Regeneration II for 0:45.
  • Dropped by Yggdrasil leaves (25% of the time) and found in its hollow.

Blocks

Yggdrasil Log

Block

  • A log: it burns, an axe breaks it fastest, and it is in the logs tag.

Yggdrasil Leaves

Block

  • Leaves that do not decay. They drop a leaf or a fruit, and now and then a gold mote drifts from the underside.

Yggdrasil Rune

Block

  • A glowing gold rune set in the world tree’s bark: light level 12, and it sheds sparks. Axe-mineable; found on Yggdrasil, not crafted.

Rune Stone

Block

  • Craft four from 4 stone bricks and 1 gold nugget. Found building the shrine and the rune circle.

Status: the items load, their icons show in a Minecraft 1.21.4 client, and the numbers above are read from the mod’s own data file. Using them (the lightning, the horn, the spear’s glow) and brewing the Blessing are not yet tested in a world.
