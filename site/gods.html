The six gods

Six pillars, six gods, six draughts. Each page has the potion, its effects and durations, the ingredient and its lore, and the recipe.

The photographs are close-ups of the test-world pillars. The epithets, domains and lore lines are the mod’s own flavour text, not scholarship. The potion effects are game design.

Colours

Each god has one colour, taken from Minecraft’s dye colours: Thor light blue, Frigg white, Heimdall orange, Odin magenta, Freyja pink, Loki lime. The colour is never the only cue: every god also has a name and an emblem. The smallest difference between any two god colours (CIE76) is ΔE 44.1, between Odin / Freyja. The build fails below 30.
