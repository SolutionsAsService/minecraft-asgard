ASGARD

The Norse world in Minecraft: six gods as potions, Mjölnir and two more relics, and structures to find them in.

Registered in code and loaded on a real server. Brewing, using the relics and natural structure spawning are not yet tested.

How a draught is made

In a brewing stand, put an Awkward Potion (the base potion: a water bottle brewed with Nether Wart, a plant item from the Nether) in a bottle slot and one god’s ingredient on top. Each god has one ingredient, one potion, and a line of lore on the ingredient’s tooltip.

The picture shows Thor’s Valor. The bottles are illustrations, not captures from the game, and the potion’s bottle colour here is a design choice. A brew in a world is not yet tested.

In the game

Captured from a Minecraft 1.21.4 client joined to a Fabric dedicated server with this mod. The pillars, the arch, the item frames and the signs were built with server commands in a flat test world; only the items and potions are the mod.

Out in the world

Captured in a flat test world on a Minecraft 1.21.4 client; the structures and items are the mod, the grass and sky are vanilla.
